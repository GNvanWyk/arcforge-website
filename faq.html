Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on quotes, payment, turnaround and finishing, based on our Terms & Conditions of Trade.

1 Getting a Quote

What files do you need from me?

Quotes and jobs are based on customer-supplied CAD/DXF files or agreed written specifications. Files are evaluated and cut strictly “as-is”, and we reserve the right to reject defective files and ask you to correct them.

Design revisions or file clean-ups requested after final sign-off incur additional CAD/nesting fees. You can send your files through our quote form.

How long is a quote valid for?

All quotations are valid for 14 calendar days from the date of issue, due to fluctuations in raw material (steel) supply costs. Prices not accepted within this period may be revised without notice.

A quotation is deemed accepted once you confirm in writing (email, WhatsApp or signature) and pay the deposit or full payment required on the quotation.

Can you help if I don’t have a CAD/DXF file?

Yes. We can perform CAD repair, vectorization or nesting at an hourly rate, quoted separately before work begins.

If technical errors in a file cannot be resolved, CAD fees already charged for that attempt are refunded in full; successfully completed CAD repair work is non-refundable.

2 Payment & Deposits

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. Our standard deposit is a non-refundable 50% of the quoted price, payable upon drawing/nesting approval, before raw material is purchased or cutting commences. The deposit percentage for your job is always stated on your quotation.

Cut-only orders (no welding, fabrication or finishing) where we supply the steel are paid 100% upfront on acceptance. Where the cost of material and outsourced work (such as galvanising or powder coating) is more than the standard deposit would cover, the deposit is increased to cover these costs.

Any remaining balance is due in full via EFT prior to, or upon, collection or dispatch of finished parts. Approved trade account clients may be offered different payment terms, but only as agreed with us in writing.

Once a deposit or upfront payment has been made, the order is confirmed and the payment is non-refundable, as it secures materials and workshop time. Only if you cancel before material has been cut, we will deduct the cost of materials and work already committed from the payment and refund any remainder at our discretion.

What payment methods do you accept?

The balance is paid via EFT, and bank details are provided on the quotation and invoice. No goods are released from the workshop until cleared funds reflect in the ArcForge Designs bank account.

When does work actually start on my order?

Turnaround commences only once your deposit or upfront payment reflects and the final DXF drawing is signed off. Raw material is purchased and cutting begins after the deposit or upfront payment stated on your quotation has been paid and the drawing/nesting approved.

3 Turnaround & Collection

How long does a typical job take?

Standard turnaround is typically 3-5 business days, commencing only once the deposit or upfront payment reflects and the final DXF drawing is signed off. Lead times are confirmed per job and may vary with workshop capacity and material availability.

Circumstances beyond our reasonable control, such as load-shedding, supplier shortages, extreme weather, civil unrest or equipment failure, can also affect lead times. We will let you know as soon as reasonably possible.

Do you deliver, or is it collection only?

Both. Collection is by appointment at the ArcForge workshop upon completion notice. Delivery can be arranged at an additional fee on request.

What happens if I don’t collect my order on time?

Completed orders not collected or accepted for delivery within 14 calendar days of completion notice may incur a storage fee of R100 per day.

4 Materials & Finishing

What materials and thicknesses can you cut?

Our CNC plasma cutter handles:

  • Mild steel: 3mm – 20mm
  • Stainless steel (304 & 316): 3mm – 16mm (20mm edge start only)
  • Aluminium: 3mm – 16mm (20mm edge start only)
  • Tread (checkered) plate: 3mm – 20mm

Custom stock gauges are available on request. Parts are cut to standard CNC thermal plasma tolerances appropriate to plate thickness. See our capabilities for more detail.

Is deburring or finishing included?

Only where explicitly itemised on your quote. Tab removal, deburring and surface finishing are not included otherwise, and minor manual touch-ups or tab removal then remain your responsibility.

Standard dross, mill scale, minor edge bevel and pierce marks are inherent to the plasma cutting process and are not defects.

Do you do welding as well as cutting?

Yes. Welding is carried out to standard workshop practice for the material and application specified. Any structural, load-bearing or certified-weld requirements must be disclosed in writing before work begins.

5 Ownership & Files

Who owns the offcuts and scrap material?

Quotations are calculated on the physical material footprint of the ordered components only. Unless you explicitly purchase the full raw sheet footprint, standard offcuts and skeleton scrap remain the property of ArcForge Designs.

Do you keep my design files, and for how long?

Where we create or modify a design, drawing or nesting layout for you, ownership of that work passes to you once it has been paid for in full. We may retain copies for record-keeping and quality-control purposes.

Our Privacy Policy sets out how long we keep files: customer files and drawings for completed jobs are kept for 24 months, and quotations that do not proceed for 12 months.

For full details, see our Terms & Conditions of Trade.
