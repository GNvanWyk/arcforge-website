Our Core Solutions

Services & Industrial Capabilities

From digital CAD geometry to high-amperage plasma arc cutting and post-processing fabrication, we deliver ready-to-weld, bolt-together metal parts.

CNC Plasma Cutting

Precision sheet metal and plate cutting directly from your DXF or vector CAD files. Clean cut edges, minimal dross, and consistent dimensional accuracy across all profiles.

  • Mild Steel from 1mm up to 16mm plate (20mm on request)
  • Stainless Steel (304 & 316) & Aluminum
  • CNC Automatic Torch Height Control (THC)
  • Clean holes, mounting slots & tight radii
Cut Parts With Us →

CAD & Vectorization

File preparation, DXF cleanup, and custom part design from hand sketches, card templates, or raw ideas. We ensure proper kerf width compensation and cut path continuity.

  • DXF / DWG / SVG file cleanup & repair
  • Reverse engineering from paper or broken parts
  • Automated sheet nesting for minimal waste
  • Decorative sign & architectural screening vectors
Prepare My Files →

Fabrication & Assembly

Downstream metalworking done in-house: MIG/TIG welding, painting, grinding, deburring and beveling. Powder coating, galvanising and other specialist finishes are available through our fabrication partners.

  • Structural MIG & precision TIG welding
  • Edge deburring, dross removal & radius beveling
  • Tapping, drilling, countersinking & slotting
  • Industrial painting in-house; powder coating & galvanising through our partners

Technical Benchmark

Shop Capabilities & Cutting Specifications

Our CNC plasma cutting setup is calibrated for clean pierce initiation, consistent kerf compensation, and minimized heat-affected zones (HAZ).

Supported Design Formats

DXF (preferred), DWG, SVG and dimensioned PDF drawings. Photos or dimensioned sketches accepted for our design service.

Cutting Equipment

CNC gantry plasma cutter with a 2,600 x 6,000 mm cutting bed and a Hypertherm 100A plasma source (air plasma, 1–16mm, up to 20mm on request).

Material Inventory

Mild steel, stainless steel (304/316), aluminium plate and checkered tread plate. Bring your own material or ask about availability.

Straightforward Workflow

How It Works: From DXF to Cut Metal

Fast, transparent, and built for speed. Get your custom parts produced in four simple steps.

01
Step One

Submit Your Design

Upload your DXF, DWG, SVG, dimensioned PDF, or dimensioned hand sketch via our online form.

02
Step Two

Material & Quote

We inspect file geometry, calculate sheet nesting, and provide a fast, comprehensive quote breakdown.

03
Step Three

Cutting & Fabrication

Our CNC plasma cuts each part with tight kerf control, followed by deburring, welding and finishing as required.

04
Step Four

Delivery / Pickup

Components are quality checked, finished to your drawing, packed securely, and dispatched or ready for workshop collection.

Once you approve the drawings and quote, a deposit confirms your order before material is purchased or cutting begins. All work is carried out under our Terms & Conditions of Trade.
